# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the Omni.Blazor community welcoming, safe, and equitable for
everyone.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects the dignity, rights,
and contributions of all individuals, regardless of characteristics including
race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity,
disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation,
language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socio-economic
position, level of education, or other status. The same privileges of
participation are extended to everyone who participates in good faith and in
accordance with this Code of Conduct.

## Encouraged Behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, backgrounds, cultures, and
native languages, we agree to act mindfully toward each other and to:

1. Respect the purpose of the project, its community spaces, and its ways of
   collaborating.
2. Engage kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
6. Commit to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Act in ways that promote and sustain the well-being of the community.

## Restricted Behaviors

Instances, threats, and promotion of the following behaviors violate this Code
of Conduct:

1. **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries or continuing
   unnecessary personal attention after a clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Directing insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments
   at a community member or group.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterizing a person's behavior or
   personality on the basis of identity or immutable traits.
4. **Sexualization.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered
   inappropriately intimate in the context of the community.
5. **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on another person's private
   information without permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other harm
   toward any person or group.
7. **Threatening community well-being.** Behaving in other ways that undermine a
   safe, healthy, and collaborative environment.

The following are also prohibited:

1. Impersonating someone else or using a misleading identity to evade
   enforcement.
2. Failing to credit the sources of contributed content.
3. Sharing marketing or commercial material outside the norms and purpose of
   the project.
4. Presenting content that contains, links to, or describes restricted behavior
   without appropriate context and care.

## Reporting an Issue

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation, but possible violations
should be reported promptly and privately. Email the Omni.Blazor maintainer at
[andersonafn@gmail.com](mailto:andersonafn@gmail.com) with the subject
`[CODE OF CONDUCT] Omni.Blazor`.

Include a description of the incident, where and when it occurred, relevant
links or records, and any additional context that may help the investigation.
Do not open a public issue for a Code of Conduct report.

The maintainer will review reports in good faith, investigate available
evidence, and prioritize the safety, privacy, and confidentiality of everyone
involved. Enforcement actions are handled privately with the involved parties,
although a mutually agreed resolution may include a public communication.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

When an investigation finds a violation, the maintainer may apply the following
enforcement ladder according to the incident's severity and impact. Steps may be
skipped when necessary to protect the community.

### 1. Warning

- **Event:** A single violation or a related series of incidents.
- **Consequence:** A private written warning that explains the violation and the
  expected behavior.
- **Repair:** Acknowledging responsibility, seeking clarification, and offering
  an appropriate apology or other corrective action.

### 2. Temporarily Limited Activities

- **Event:** A repeated violation after a warning or a more serious first
  violation.
- **Consequence:** A time-limited restriction on specified community channels or
  interactions.
- **Repair:** Respecting the restriction, reflecting on the impact, and returning
  to community spaces thoughtfully after the restriction ends.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

- **Event:** Repeated violations that warnings have not resolved, or a single
  serious violation.
- **Consequence:** Temporary removal from community spaces, with conditions for
  return.
- **Repair:** Respecting the suspension and meeting the stated conditions before
  rejoining the community.

### 4. Permanent Ban

- **Event:** A serious violation that makes continued participation unsafe, or a
  pattern of violations that prior enforcement has not resolved.
- **Consequence:** Permanent removal from project community spaces and
  communication channels.
- **Repair:** No return to the community is available at this level.

This ladder is a guideline and does not prevent the maintainer from applying
reasonable judgment in the best interests of the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Omni.Blazor community spaces, including the
repository, issues, pull requests, code review, and other official project
channels. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in
public or private spaces.
